### 2. NEW STAT SYSTEM & "^" DROPDOWN UI
1. **Character Stat Schema:**
   Every morphed character uses these 11 stats:
   - `HP`: Max health
   - `Regen`: HP regenerated per second
   - `Stamina`: Max stamina
   - `Recovery`: Stamina regenerated per second
   - `Walk`: Base walk speed
   - `Sprint`: Sprint speed when holding LeftShift
   - `Physical`: Physical attack power
   - `Magic`: Magic attack power
   - `DEF`: Flat damage barrier that reduces ALL incoming Physical/Magic damage by `DEF` (`FinalDamage = math.max(0, RawDamage - TargetDEF)`). Only `TRUE` damage bypasses `DEF`.
   - `Crit`: Critical hit chance percentage (0 to 100)
   - `CritDMG`: Critical damage multiplier percentage (`100` = `1.0x` base multiplier, calculated as `CritDMG / 100`).

2. **UI Stat Panel (`^` Button):**
   - Add a `"^"` toggle button directly above the HP/Stamina bar HUD.
   - Clicking `"^"` toggles open/closed a clean panel listing all 11 current stats (`HP`, `Regen`, `Stamina`, `Recovery`, `Walk`, `Sprint`, `Physical`, `Magic`, `DEF`, `Crit`, `Crit DMG`) and updates dynamically when the player morphs.
