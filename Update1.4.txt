### 3. FIX GROT "VINE" (`E` SKILL) — FIX 90° SIDEWAYS / FLOATING BUG & USE REAL `"Skill area"` MODELS
- **Why the Vine was facing 90° sideways and floating in the air:**
  In `Workspace.Grot`, `Grot_hitbox` inside `Grot_S1frame1..5` has its local CFrame axes rotated 90° compared to `HumanoidRootPart.CFrame.LookVector`. Using raw `frameHitbox.CFrame:ToObjectSpace(part.CFrame)` caused the vine strip to stick out sideways at 90° and tilt into the sky!
- **Exact Mathematical Fix (Use This Direction-Normalized Basis):**
  1. Each frame model (`Grot_S1frame1`, `Grot_S1frame2`, `Grot_S1frame3`, `Grot_S1frame4`, `Grot_S1frame5`) has its own `"Grot_hitbox"` part and its own child `Model` named `"Skill area"` (containing the 3 grass strips and rock spikes).
  2. For each frame `1..5`, compute the true forward direction from `"Grot_hitbox"` to `"Skill area"` in Studio space:
     ```lua
     local frameHitbox = frameModel:FindFirstChild("Grot_hitbox", true)
     local skillArea = frameModel:FindFirstChild("Skill area")
     local areaCF, areaSize = skillArea:GetBoundingBox()
     -- Direction from Grot_hitbox to Skill area on the flat X-Z plane in Studio:
     local studioForward = Vector3.new(areaCF.Position.X - frameHitbox.Position.X, 0, areaCF.Position.Z - frameHitbox.Position.Z).Unit
     if studioForward.Magnitude < 0.01 then studioForward = Vector3.new(0, 0, -1) end
     local studioBasisCF = CFrame.lookAt(frameHitbox.Position, frameHitbox.Position + studioForward, Vector3.yAxis)
     ```
  3. Store every `BasePart` inside `skillArea:GetDescendants()` relative to `studioBasisCF`:
     ```lua
     local relCF = studioBasisCF:ToObjectSpace(part.CFrame)
     ```
  4. When spawning or updating the `"Skill area"` in-game in front of Grot's live `HumanoidRootPart` (`hrp`):
     ```lua
     local playerFlatLook = Vector3.new(hrp.CFrame.LookVector.X, 0, hrp.CFrame.LookVector.Z).Unit
     local playerBasisCF = CFrame.lookAt(hrp.Position, hrp.Position + playerFlatLook, Vector3.yAxis)
     clonedPart.CFrame = playerBasisCF * relCF
     ```
     - Do the **exact same `studioBasisCF` calculation** for `Grot_S1interhitbox` -> `Grot_S1hitbox`!
     - This guarantees the 3 grass lanes and rock spikes ALWAYS lie 100% flat on the floor and point 100% straight forward in the direction Grot is looking—never sideways and never floating!
- **Exact Wind-Up (`0.95s`) & Active Zone (`3.0s`) Behavior:**
